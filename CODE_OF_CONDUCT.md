# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, research traditions and experience levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive, specific feedback
- Crediting others' work accurately and citing sources
- Focusing on what is best for the research community

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Plagiarism, fabricated contributions, or misrepresenting others' work
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers via the contact on the Kalaris Labs GitHub
organization profile, or by opening a confidential security advisory if the matter is
sensitive. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy is respected.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior violates this code.
