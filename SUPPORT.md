# Support

- **Questions, ideas, show-and-tell:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/KalarisLabs/research-agent-skills/discussions)
- **Bugs in a skill, script or the installer:** [open an issue](https://github.com/KalarisLabs/research-agent-skills/issues/new/choose). Include `npx research-agent-skills doctor` output.
- **Request a skill for your field:** use the *Skill request* issue template.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** never in public, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Documentation:** https://docs.kalarislabs.com/

Maintained by Sayan Chowdhury ([@saynchowdhury](https://github.com/saynchowdhury)) at Kalaris Labs.
We aim to triage new issues within two business days.
