# `abstract-and-title`

**Category:** [research-writing](/skills#research-writing) · **License:** MIT · **Version:** 1.0

**Tags:** abstract, title, keywords, highlights, significance statement, lay summary, scientific writing

## When to use it

Write and sharpen research paper titles, abstracts (structured and unstructured), keywords, highlights, significance statements, graphical-abstract text and lay summaries. Use when drafting or revising an abstract to a word limit, turning results into a one-sentence contribution, choosing between candidate titles, writing Cell/Elsevier highlights, a PNAS significance statement or a plain-language summary, or optimizing discoverability (keywords, search terms).

## Example requests

Ask your agent in plain language, for example:

- *"Cut my 320-word abstract to 250 words for PLOS ONE and suggest three better titles."*
