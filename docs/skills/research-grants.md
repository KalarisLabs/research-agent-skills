# `research-grants`

**Category:** [research-writing](/skills#research-writing) · **License:** MIT · **Version:** 1.3

**Tags:** grant writing, NIH, NSF, research proposals, funding

## When to use it

Guides writing of competitive research grant proposals for NSF, NIH, DOE, DARPA, and Taiwan NSTC. Covers agency-specific formatting and review criteria, specific aims, project descriptions, significance and innovation narratives, broader impacts, budgets and justifications, timelines, biosketches, and the NSTC CM03 form. Use when drafting a proposal for one of these agencies. Use when writing specific aims or broader impacts statements. Use when preparing a budget justification or milestone plan. Use when responding to a solicitation, BAA, or reviewer comments in a resubmission. Use when checking a draft against page limits and submission requirements. For general manuscript writing, use a scientific writing skill instead.

## Example requests

Ask your agent in plain language, for example:

- *"Help me write the Specific Aims page for my R01 on gut microbiome modulation of anti-PD1 response."*
- *"I'm submitting to NSF CBET and my draft is in proposal_v3.docx. The Project Description is 17 pages and my Broader Impacts section is just one paragraph about 'training students'. How do I fix this?"*
- *"My lab got a summary statement with a score of 38 on our first NIH submission and I need to turn it around for the next cycle. Reviewers said the approach was underpowered and the innovation was incremental. What's my plan?"*
